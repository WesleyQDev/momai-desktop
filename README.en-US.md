# MomAI Desktop

MomAI Desktop lets your assistant open programs, folders, and files on your computer. You can also ask it to click and type on buttons by name, so you don't have to find the path yourself.

## What you can do

- Open installed programs by name: browser, Spotify, text editor, or calculator.
- Open system folders such as Downloads, Documents, and Pictures.
- Click a button or fill a field by name. For example: "click Save".
- See every automation step in a chat card and the full timeline on the extension page.
- Take a screenshot of the current screen, another monitor, or a window. The image becomes a file and only appears in chat when you ask for it. You can show it, keep it, or attach it to an email.
- If more than one program has a similar name, MomAI asks which one to open.
- Choose which apps can open, approve each click, and stop an automation at any time.

## Examples

- "Open Chrome."
- "Start Spotify."
- "Open the Downloads folder."
- "Click Save in Notepad."
- "Type my email in the login field."
- "Take a screenshot of the Notepad window and show it to me."
- "What have you done so far?"

## Privacy

The extension only acts when you ask. Screenshots, replay captures, and preferences stay on your computer, and old screenshots are deleted over time. When you ask for a screen description, the image goes to the vision model active in MomAI and comes back as text. Your user account permissions still apply.

## Permissions

- **System:** to open programs, folders, and windows and to perform the clicks and typing you ask for.
- **Files:** to open folders and save screenshots.
- **Network:** to send the image to MomAI's vision model when you ask for a description.
